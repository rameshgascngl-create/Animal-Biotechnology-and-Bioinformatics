Pairwise Alignment and Dot Plots

Alignment arranges sequences to reveal homology: similarity due to common ancestry.

Homology vs similarity
Sequences are homologous if they share a common ancestor (yes or no); similarity is a measurable percentage. Never write “70% homology”.
Fig. 4. A dot plot compares two sequences visually.
Fig. 5. Dynamic programming fills a matrix; the traceback gives the alignment.
GlobalLocal
AlgorithmNeedleman–Wunsch (1970)Smith–Waterman (1981)
AlignsEntire length of both sequencesBest matching sub-regions
UseClosely related, same-length sequencesDomains, distant homologues, database searching
ToolsEMBOSS Needle, ClustalEMBOSS Water, BLAST (heuristic)

PG focus

  • Substitution matrices: PAM (evolutionary distance) and BLOSUM62 (clustered blocks; default in BLAST).
  • Affine gap penalty: gap opening plus extension, penalising long gaps less per position.

BLAST trades the full dynamic-programming guarantee for speed by seeding with short words.

Fig. 7. Reading the E-value.

Reading a BLAST report

  • Query cover: fraction of query aligned. % identity: matches in the aligned region. Bit score: normalised, database-independent. E-value: lower is more significant.
  • A high identity over a short stretch can be less convincing than 40% over a full domain.

📋 Summary

Summary & Exam Revision

Key points and model answers likely to appear in university examinations.

Key exam points
1
INSDC: GenBank, ENA and DDBJ share data; UniProt (Swiss-Prot reviewed, TrEMBL unreviewed); PDB for structures.
2
FASTA: > header + sequence; FASTQ adds Phred quality scores.
3
Homology is yes/no; similarity and identity are percentages.
4
Needleman–Wunsch = global; Smith–Waterman = local; both use dynamic programming.
5
PAM and BLOSUM62 are substitution matrices; affine gap penalties.
6
BLAST: seed words, extension, HSP, bit score, E-value; BLASTN/P/X/TBLASTN/TBLASTX.
7
Lower E-value means more significant; 1e-5 is a common threshold.
8
MSA reveals conserved residues; guide trees order progressive alignments.
9
Protein structure: primary, secondary, tertiary and quaternary; PDB files list ATOM records.
10
AlphaFold predicts structures with confidence scores (pLDDT).
Short-answer questions

Click a question to reveal the model answer.

Q.1Differentiate primary and secondary databases.▼

Primary databases hold raw data submitted by researchers (GenBank, PDB); secondary databases hold curated or derived information (Swiss-Prot, Pfam, PROSITE, RefSeq).

Q.2What is FASTA format?▼

A text format with a header line starting with “>” followed by the sequence in one or more lines.

Q.3Distinguish global and local alignment.▼

Global (Needleman–Wunsch) aligns whole sequences; local (Smith–Waterman) finds the best matching regions.

Q.4Explain how BLAST works.▼

Query split into words; neighbourhood words found; database scanned for seeds; hits extended into HSPs; scores converted to bit scores and E-values.

Q.5What does an E-value of 1e–25 mean?▼

Only 1e–25 matches of that score are expected by chance in a database of that size, so the hit is highly significant.

Q.6Write short notes on the PDB.▼

Archive of experimentally determined 3-D structures (X-ray, NMR, cryo-EM) with a 4-character ID; ATOM records give x, y, z coordinates.

Q.7List applications of bioinformatics.▼

Gene finding, variant calling, phylogenetics, drug design, structure prediction, transcriptomics, breeding and CRISPR guide design.

📖 Glossary

Key Terms & Glossary

Important terms in simple language.

INSDC
International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration.
Accession number
Stable identifier for a database record.
Homology
Similarity due to common ancestry.
HSP
High-scoring segment pair found by BLAST.
E-value
Expected number of chance hits with equal or better score.
Bit score
Normalised alignment score.
Orthologue
Homologous gene separated by speciation.
Paralogue
Homologous gene separated by duplication.
Contig
Contiguous assembled sequence from overlapping reads.
Phred score
Quality value Q = −10 log₁₀ P(error).
Clade
Ancestor and all its descendants.
pLDDT
AlphaFold per-residue confidence (0–100).
