# Real-phone test sheet

**Not verified on a physical device.** Use an authorised candidate APK with a recorded commit and signer. Use a spare phone or test profile for clean install and restore, and keep the source device’s existing records intact. The current backup expectation is **option A**. All Tamil text shown by this candidate is draft.

Record the build commit and artifact, APK signer, version, phone model, Android version, font scale, TalkBack version and result for each row. For a failure, record the action, actual result and screenshot or TalkBack announcement.

| # | Student action | Expected result |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Fresh install on the test device. Turn off network access and open the app. | Home opens in default English, with the syllabus available and no sign-in or network step. |
| 2 | From Home, change to Tamil and open a lesson. Switch back to English and then to Tamil. | Home and the reader display the selected language. Known scientific symbols can be retained. The lesson is still usable after switching. |
| 3 | Open a syllabus topic that shares a chapter with another topic, such as a separate Cell Culture syllabus item. | The intended topic opens at its own section on first visit. Back returns to Home. |
| 4 | Tap a lesson figure, pinch in, pan to a label, then use Reset. | The full-screen viewer opens, the label can be inspected, Reset returns to fit view and Back closes the viewer. |
| 5 | Reopen the figure, zoom, and rotate the device. Return to portrait and go Back. | The viewer remains open, keeps approximately its place and scale, and Back returns to the same lesson position. |
| 6 | Star a topic and mark it studied. Open it from Saved. | The chosen topic appears in Saved. Its studied marker and its lesson are available. |
| 7 | In Tamil mode, use Search on a distinctive Tamil heading visible in the lesson, then open the matching result. | The matching chapter and section are identifiable in the results and the selected result opens that section. |
| 8 | Open Tamil Revise, choose a chapter and reveal one short answer. | The chapter title, key points, question and revealed answer contain meaningful Tamil text and the answer is legible. |
| 9 | Open Tamil Glossary and search for a visible Tamil headword. | The term and its definition are shown in Tamil for the selected match. |
| 10 | Open Tamil Flashcards, show the answer, then mark it for review or remembered as offered. | The prompt and answer show Tamil text, and the next card or session summary appears. |
| 11 | Finish a chapter quiz and return to Quiz. | Answers show feedback, a result appears, and the chapter’s best score is shown where applicable. |
| 12 | Leave a lesson partway through. Force-stop, relaunch offline and choose Continue reading. | The saved language, lesson and reading position are restored on the same device. Saved and studied marks and the quiz score persist. |
| 13 | Enable TalkBack. Swipe through Home controls, open a Tamil lesson figure, operate zoom and close the viewer. | Focus reaches actionable controls in an understandable order. The figure is described and zoom, reset, close and Back have meaningful spoken labels. |
