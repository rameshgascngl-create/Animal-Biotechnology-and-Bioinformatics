# Tamil terminology: Tamil Nadu State Board usage (for owner review)

Standing instruction (owner): use Tamil Nadu textbook Biology/Zoology terminology; school (State Board) or university text is acceptable.

**Source and its limits.** Terms marked *Board* were read from the Tamil-medium State Board **Class 12 Botany, Chapter 4
(Principles and Processes of Biotechnology)** as reproduced on brainkart.in (`Tools-for-Genetic-Engineering_38241`,
`Principles-and-Processes-of-Biotechnology--Summary_38246`). That is a third-party copy of the textbook, not the official
PDF, and it is Botany, not Zoology. Further pages of the same chapter (gene transfer, screening, glossary) were read to settle more terms. Terms marked *Unverified* are my choice where none of those pages had an equivalent. Please correct them; fixes are applied globally.

**Owner rule: where no appropriate Tamil word is found in school or university texts, keep the English term with the same phonetics (transliterate into Tamil script); do not coin Tamil words.** Phonetic transliterations that the Board itself uses (e.g. இன்ட்ரான், புரோகேரியோட்டு) stay.

Rules: enzyme names (EcoRI, Taq), organisms, chemical symbols, units and DNA sequences stay in Latin script; DNA, RNA, cDNA,
PCR, ATP stay in Latin. Markup `**bold**` and `_italic_` is preserved. Glossary headwords carry the English term in brackets
(`ta_status = bracketed`).
