# Tamil terms in figures: provenance ledger (batches 1 and 2)

Rule (R. Ramesh, 2026-11-12): use the term from the chapter that covers the technique shown; where the books give two options take that rule's choice and keep the other as a glossary synonym. Latin symbols and abbreviations stay Latin. Terms with no textbook term get a short descriptive Tamil phrase, marked here as **descriptive, not State Board**.
