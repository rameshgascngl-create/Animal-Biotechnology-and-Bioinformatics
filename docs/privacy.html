Privacy Policy

App: Animal Biotechnology (package com.gasczoology.animalbiotech)

Animal Biotechnology is an offline educational app. It has no user accounts, advertising or analytics. The app does not send your study information to the developer or third parties.

Reading positions, bookmarks, quiz scores, flashcard progress and preferences are stored on your device. The app excludes these records from Android backup and device transfer.

To delete these records, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
